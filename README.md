# The combinatorics of phylogenetic trees, in Lean

[![CI](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-combinatorics/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-combinatorics/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

Lean 4 formalizations of the combinatorics of phylogenetic trees, one paper per repository: how a
tree is determined by its metric, its clusters or its lowest common ancestors, how trees are compared,
when characters and trees are compatible, and how quartets are assembled into a tree, with the
computational complexity of these problems and the algorithms that solve them. They start with the
characterization, comparison and reconstruction of trees; continue with perfect phylogeny and the
compatibility of characters; and end with the amalgamation of quartets.

This repository is their hub, in the collection rooted at
[lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics). It holds:

- the library [`PhyloCombinatorics`](PhyloCombinatorics), for what two papers or more of this hub share and the root library
  does not hold. It has nothing yet: what they share so far (trees as hierarchies and as split families, the Splits Equivalence Theorem, tree metrics and the four-point condition, and quartets) is in the root library.
  Its [README](PhyloCombinatorics/README.md) says what goes there;
- the catalog of the papers, [`catalog/papers.toml`](catalog/papers.toml), from which the tables
  below are generated.

The [conventions](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/CONVENTIONS.md) that
every repository follows, the [workflow](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/WORKFLOW.md),
the registry of the repositories, the shared foundations and the tools are in the root. Each paper is
formalized with the [formalize-math-paper](https://github.com/vltanh/formalize-math-paper) skill:
every result the paper proves is proved by the paper's own argument, the statements are checked
against the paper, and an audit of the paper reports its errors and gaps.

## The papers

Each paper is in one family, the one its problem belongs to. This is a hub of theory: its papers
study problems on trees, characters and quartets whatever the process that generated them, and a
result about one estimator is with that estimator, in a hub of inference
([conventions](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/CONVENTIONS.md), Section
1). The papers of family 1 are foundations of the whole collection: their reusable results go to the
root library, and each paper's repository formalizes the rest. *Uses* lists the papers, of any hub,
that the paper's proofs are known to use, and the other hubs whose library it requires. Each paper's
inventory completes the list.

## Starting a paper

From [lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics), beside this repository,
when the paper's formalization starts:

```sh
python3 scripts/status.py next        # the open papers, by priority, with what each waits on
python3 scripts/new_paper.py aho1981  # creates ../lean4-aho1981 on the train, under this hub
cd ../lean4-aho1981 && claude         # then: /formalize-math-paper https://doi.org/10.1137/0210030
```

## Building

```sh
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake build Phylogenetics
lake env lean scripts/Audit.lean
```

Lean and Mathlib `v4.37.0-rc1`; [`lakefile.toml`](lakefile.toml) requires lean4-phylogenetics by
commit, `3fcabfd`. The audit, [`scripts/Audit.lean`](scripts/Audit.lean), checks that every
declaration of this library and of lean4-phylogenetics depends only on Lean's standard axioms
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`; `python3 scripts/gen_audit.py` generates its
`import all` lines. CI runs the build and the audit, and checks that the tables above are current.
